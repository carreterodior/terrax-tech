Privacy Policy.

The TERRAX TECH app works entirely offline. It collects nothing about you, and nothing it stores ever leaves your phone.

Effective August 6, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The TERRAX TECH app does not collect, store on servers, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network features.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth Low Energy solely to discover and control your TERRAX accessories, such as rock lights, light strips, interior ambient lighting, and electric running boards.

Bluetooth communication happens directly between your phone and the accessory. It never passes through a server and never leaves your device.

Data stored on your device

Your saved accessories, the names and categories you give them, and your app settings are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Subscriptions

TERRAX Pro is an optional subscription sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details, and no purchase information is sent to us or to any third party.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
