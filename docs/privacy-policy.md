# TERRAX TECH Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

TERRAX TECH ("the app") is a companion app for TERRAX vehicle accessories.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on servers, transmit, or share any
personal information or usage data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no network features.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth Low Energy solely to discover and control your TERRAX
accessories (lighting and running boards) nearby. Bluetooth communication
happens directly between your phone and the accessory and never leaves your
device.

## Data stored on your device

Your saved accessories, their names, categories, and app settings are stored
only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
