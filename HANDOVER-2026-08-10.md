## 4. The agreed pre-upload improvement slate ("1.1")

Agreed with Dior on Aug 10, not yet started. Bugs first:

1. **Ambient-light relight delay (~20 s)**: retry frames the unit NACKs as
   busy (`0xFC`) right after wake, with backoff. Unit-test against recorded
   NACK codes; confirm on hardware later.
2. **Pause is a silent no-op on module-B running boards** (`IntelligoDriver.
   stop()` intentionally does nothing there): add a capability flag so the
   UI hides or explains the Pause button instead of lying.
3. **Wrong-driver device entries**: when connect fails with
   "characteristic not found", offer re-detection instead of relying on the
   long-press-remove-re-add folklore.

Then features: **group control** (one colour change to all lights / front+rear
rock-light sync — highest customer value), **auto-connect to saved devices on
app open**, **in-app Help page** (supported devices, "device not showing up?"
checklist, terraxgear.com/contact). Then polish: throttle section sliders like
the colour wheel, scan-screen rescan affordance, About page with version +
`showLicensePage`.
